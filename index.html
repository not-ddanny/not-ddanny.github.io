Daniel Liu is a writer. He is the recipient of the 2024 David Rice Ecker Prize and the 2023 Lin Arison Excellence in Writing Award. He was selected as a 2022 Foyle Young Poet of the Year by The Poetry Society and was a finalist for the 2022 Adroit Prize in both poetry and prose. He was a 2023 YoungArts National Finalist and has been recognized by the Pulitzer Center, Bennington College, and Columbia College Chicago. His work has appeared in Sixth Finch, The Adroit Journal, Diode, Tinderbox, and elsewhere. He is currently working on a collection of poems.

Selected Poems

  • “Television” and “Capacity” — House House Magazine
  • “Black Balsam” — Sixth Finch
  • “Return Home” and “Essay on Fortune-telling” — The Adroit Journal
  • “Wetland Theory” — Tinderbox Poetry Journal
